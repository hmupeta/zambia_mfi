This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Master Software as a Service Agreement between Nimbus Solutions Limited ("Processor") and the Client microfinance institution ("Controller"), and is entered into pursuant to the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 of the Republic of Zambia.

1. Roles and scope

The Controller determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data. The Processor processes Personal Data only on behalf of and in accordance with the documented instructions of the Controller.

2. Processing instructions

The Processor shall:

  • Process Personal Data only for the purposes of providing the Platform and Services under the Agreement;
  • Ensure that persons authorised to process Personal Data are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations;
  • Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorised access, loss, or destruction;
  • Maintain accurate records of all processing activities.

3. Sub-processors

The Processor shall not engage any sub-processor without prior written authorisation from the Controller. Where a sub-processor is engaged, the Processor shall impose data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA.

4. Data subject rights

The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to data subject requests to access, rectify, or delete Personal Data, in accordance with the DPA. Where a data subject contacts the Processor directly, the Processor shall forward the request to the Controller without undue delay.

5. Data breach notification

In the event of a Personal Data breach, the Processor shall:

  • Notify the Controller within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of the breach;
  • Provide the Controller with all information necessary to comply with its notification obligations to the Data Protection Commission and affected data subjects;
  • Cooperate fully with the Controller and any regulatory authority investigating the breach.

6. Data localisation

In accordance with the DPA, Personal Data collected and processed in Zambia shall be stored within Zambia, unless the Controller provides explicit written authorisation for transfer to a jurisdiction that provides adequate data protection safeguards.

7. Data Protection Officer

Each Party shall designate a Data Protection Officer (DPO) responsible for overseeing compliance with the DPA and shall provide the other Party with the DPO's contact details. The DPO's details shall be kept current and made available to the Data Protection Commission upon request.

8. Audit rights

The Controller shall have the right to audit the Processor's data processing activities, either directly or through an independent third party, upon thirty (30) days' written notice and no more than once per calendar year, except where an audit is required by a regulatory authority.

9. Data return and deletion

Upon termination or expiry of the Agreement, the Processor shall, at the Controller's option, return all Personal Data in a standard electronic format or securely delete all Personal Data within sixty (60) days, subject to any legal retention requirements.

10. Governing law

This DPA is governed by the laws of the Republic of Zambia. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this DPA shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the Master Software as a Service Agreement.
