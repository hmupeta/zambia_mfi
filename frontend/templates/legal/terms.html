This Master Software as a Service Agreement is entered into on the Effective Date between Nimbus Solutions Limited, a company incorporated in the Republic of Zambia ("Nimbus Solutions", "Provider", "we", "us", "our"), and the Client, being a microfinance institution registered and licensed in the Republic of Zambia ("Client", "MFI", "you", "your").

1. Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise:

  • "Applicable Law" means all laws, regulations, directives, and guidelines applicable in the Republic of Zambia, including the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act No. 4 of 2021, the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, the Information and Communications Technologies Act No. 15 of 2009, and the Competition and Consumer Protection Act No. 24 of 2010.
  • "Client Data" means all data, information, and records submitted by the Client or its customers to the Platform.
  • "Data Controller" and "Data Processor" have the meanings assigned to them under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021.
  • "Platform" means the software-as-a-service platform developed and operated by the Provider, including all modules, updates, and documentation.
  • "Subscription Term" means the period during which the Client is authorised to access and use the Platform.
  • "User" means any individual authorised by the Client to access the Platform.

2. Scope of services

The Provider shall provide the Client with access to the Platform on a subscription basis. The Platform includes modules for client onboarding and KYC, loan origination, loan servicing, savings management, reporting, and integration with mobile money and payment gateways.

The Provider grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right to access and use the Platform during the Subscription Term for the Client's internal business purposes.

3. Subscription term and renewal

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for the Initial Term set out in the applicable Order Schedule. Upon expiry, this Agreement automatically renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

4. Fees and payment

The Client shall pay the Subscription Fees set out in the applicable Order Schedule in Zambian Kwacha (ZMW). All fees are exclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT) at the rate of 16% as prescribed under the VAT Act and the VAT (Cross Border Electronic Services) Regulations, 2024.

Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at a rate of 2% per month, calculated from the due date until payment is received.

5. Client obligations

The Client shall:

  • Ensure that each User is aware of and complies with the terms of this Agreement;
  • Maintain the confidentiality of all User credentials;
  • Promptly notify the Provider of any unauthorised use of the Platform or security breach;
  • Comply with all Applicable Laws governing its microfinance operations, including regulations issued by the Bank of Zambia.

6. Data protection and privacy

For the purposes of the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, the Client is the Data Controller and the Provider is the Data Processor. The Provider processes Personal Data only on behalf of and in accordance with the documented instructions of the Client.

In the event of a Personal Data breach, the Provider shall notify the Client within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of the breach, and shall cooperate fully with the Client and any regulatory authority investigating the breach.

In accordance with the DPA, Personal Data collected and processed in Zambia shall be stored within Zambia, unless the Client provides explicit written authorisation for transfer to a jurisdiction that provides adequate data protection safeguards.

7. Intellectual property rights

All intellectual property rights in the Platform, including all software, source code, documentation, and any modifications or enhancements, are and shall remain the sole and exclusive property of the Provider. The Client retains all intellectual property rights in the Client Data.

8. Service level agreement

The Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.5%, excluding Scheduled Maintenance, Emergency Maintenance, Force Majeure Events, and Downtime caused by the Client's systems or internet connectivity.

9. Confidentiality

Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence, shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, and shall use it solely for the purposes of performing its obligations under this Agreement. These obligations survive termination for a period of five (5) years, except for trade secrets, which shall remain confidential in perpetuity.

10. Representations and warranties

Each Party represents and warrants that it has full legal capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement and that the execution and performance of this Agreement do not violate any Applicable Law or any other agreement to which it is a party.

Except as expressly set forth in this Agreement, the Platform and Services are provided "as is", and the Provider disclaims all other warranties, express or implied, to the maximum extent permitted by Applicable Law.

11. Indemnification

The Provider shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Client from any claims arising from a claim that the Platform infringes a third party's intellectual property rights, the Provider's breach of its data protection obligations, or the Provider's gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

The Client shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Provider from any claims arising from the Client's use of the Platform in violation of this Agreement or Applicable Law, the accuracy of Client Data, or the Client's breach of its regulatory obligations as a microfinance institution.

12. Limitation of liability

Except for a Party's indemnification obligations, a Party's breach of confidentiality, or the Client's payment obligations, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the total Subscription Fees paid by the Client in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the liability.

To the maximum extent permitted by Applicable Law, neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for any loss of profits, revenue, data, or goodwill.

13. Force majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations (other than payment obligations) to the extent caused by a Force Majeure Event. If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than ninety (90) consecutive days, either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

14. Term and termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice, becomes insolvent or enters liquidation, or ceases to hold any licence or registration required to perform its obligations.

Upon termination, the Client's right to access the Platform shall cease and the Provider shall return or delete Client Data in accordance with the data protection provisions of this Agreement.

15. Governing law and dispute resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Zambia. Any dispute shall first be subject to good-faith negotiations, then mediation in Lusaka in accordance with the rules of the Zambia Centre for Dispute Resolution, and finally arbitration in Lusaka in accordance with the Arbitration Act, Cap. 40 of the Laws of Zambia.

16. General provisions

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings. No amendment shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both Parties. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party. Electronic signatures shall be deemed valid in accordance with the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act No. 4 of 2021.
