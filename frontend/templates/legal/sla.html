This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") forms part of the Master Software as a Service Agreement between Nimbus Solutions Limited ("Provider") and the Client microfinance institution ("Client").

1. Availability commitment

The Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available twenty-four (24) hours per day, seven (7) days per week, with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.5%, excluding:

  • Scheduled Maintenance;
  • Emergency Maintenance;
  • Force Majeure Events;
  • Downtime caused by the Client's systems, equipment, or internet connectivity.

2. Service credits

Service Credits shall be the Client's sole and exclusive remedy for the Provider's failure to meet the availability commitment.

4. Support channels

Email, telephone, and in-app chat during Business Hours (08:00 to 17:00 CAT, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in Zambia).

5. Scheduled maintenance

The Provider shall perform scheduled maintenance during the window of 02:00 to 05:00 CAT on Sundays, with at least forty-eight (48) hours' prior notice. Scheduled maintenance windows are excluded from uptime calculations.

6. Emergency maintenance

The Provider may perform emergency maintenance at any time with as much prior notice as is reasonably practicable. Emergency maintenance is excluded from uptime calculations where it is required to prevent or mitigate a critical security or availability incident.

7. Exclusions

Support does not cover issues arising from:

  • The Client's misuse of the Platform;
  • Modifications not authorised by the Provider;
  • Third-party integrations not approved by the Provider;
  • Client-side network, browser, or device failures.

8. Governing law

This SLA is governed by the laws of the Republic of Zambia. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this SLA shall be resolved in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the Master Software as a Service Agreement.
